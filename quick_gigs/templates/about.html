About SkillMatch AI

Our Mission

SkillMatch AI is on a mission to democratize access to employment opportunities by connecting local workers with immediate job opportunities. We believe everyone deserves a fair chance to earn a livelihood with dignity.

Founded in 2025, SkillMatch AI helps workers find temporary and permanent jobs while assisting employers in finding reliable help for their needs.

SDG 1 — No Poverty

SkillMatch AI supports SDG 1 by helping people discover suitable jobs, identify missing skills, access free learning resources, and improve their employment and earning opportunities.

The tagline, "Connecting Skills to Opportunities and Building Pathways Out of Poverty," reflects our goal to help job seekers, fresh graduates, daily-wage workers, and underemployed people find practical opportunities and pathways to better livelihoods.

This initiative is aligned with the United Nations SDG 1 reference at https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal1 and does not claim official affiliation with the United Nations.

SDG 1 — Opportunities for Everyone SDG 1 Impact

Our Vision

We envision a world where no one is unemployed due to lack of access to job opportunities. Through technology, we aim to bridge the gap between job seekers and providers in local communities.

Our platform is designed specifically for rural and semi-urban areas where traditional job portals often fail to reach.

How It Works

For Job Seekers: Browse available gigs in your area, apply with one click, and start earning. No complicated resumes needed.

For Job Providers: Post your job requirements, connect with verified local workers, and get your work done efficiently.

Safety & Trust

At SkillMatch AI, we prioritize the safety and security of both job seekers and employers. Our platform includes:

  • User verification through phone and ID checks
  • Secure messaging system for communication
  • Rating and review system for accountability
  • Customer support for any issues
  • Clear guidelines for fair pay and working conditions
